Museum After Dark

How to play

As an exhibit

  • Move while the guard is looking somewhere else.
  • Let go of the controls to freeze. If a flashlight lands on you while you're moving, you're caught.
  • Find every gem, then get back on your pedestal before sunrise at 6:00 AM.
  • Running makes noise. Tiptoe when a guard is close.
  • On a phone, drag anywhere to sneak. A gentle drag tiptoes. On a computer, use WASD or the arrow keys and hold Shift to tiptoe. With a controller, tilt the left stick a little to tiptoe.

As the night guard

  • You can only see what your flashlight lights up.
  • The exhibits are trying to carry gems back to their pedestals. Catch one moving in your light and its gem goes back. Three catches and that exhibit is out.
  • A carried gem twinkles in the dark. Watch for it.
  • Rings in the dark are running footsteps. Point your light that way.
  • Staring at a frozen exhibit makes your flashlight overheat and turn off for 2 seconds.
  • Spinning around nonstop makes you dizzy for a moment.
  • On a phone, your left thumb walks and your right thumb aims. On a computer, WASD walks and the mouse aims. With a controller, the left stick walks and the right stick aims.
